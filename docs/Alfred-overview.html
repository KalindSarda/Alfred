Alfred

Your personal finance assistant. Finances first.

Alfred keeps your money in order. You tell him what you spent, earned, lent or bought, in a chat message or a quick form. He keeps every account, card, loan and investment up to date, shows what is safe to spend, and speaks up before something slips: a card bill coming due, a friend who has not repaid, an IPO result, an EMI that does not match your statement. Built for one person, ready for a household.

How you use him

Telegram chatWrite plainly: "450 swiggy hdfc yesterday". Alfred files it, and asks only what he cannot work out. Buttons for undo, follow-ups and quick answers.
The web appWorks on phone and desktop, installable like an app. Home shows a daily briefing, what is safe to spend, and shortcuts. One search finds any feature.
Daily nudgesEach morning Alfred checks what is due and messages you: card payments, repayments, IPO outcomes, instalments to confirm, and a monthly prompt to refresh investment values.

What he does today

Accounts and cardsBank accounts and credit cards with live balances, card statement and due dates, and a clear safe-to-spend figure.
TransactionsExpenses, income, transfers, card bill payments, with categories, search and filters. A one-line add remembers your usual merchants.
Net worth and trendBanks, investments and money owed to you, less cards, EMIs and what you owe, with a month-by-month chart.
Budgets and recurringMonthly limits by category, and rent, subscriptions, salary and SIPs added automatically on their day.
Savings goalsSet money aside for something. It stays in the bank but is held back from what you can spend, with the monthly amount needed.
Card EMI plansWorks out each instalment from the rate, adds the fee and GST, books each month, and asks if it matches your statement.
Blocked IPO fundsTracks money held for IPO applications, removes it from what you can spend, and asks for the result. An allotment becomes an investment.
Lent and borrowedWho owes you and whom you owe, repayments, due dates, and polite follow-ups. People you trust are never chased.
InvestmentsStocks and IPO holdings, gold, crypto and more: what you put in, what they are worth now, gain or loss, with buying and selling linked to your bank.
Yours to keepOne-click export of everything. Invite-only access, sign-in by emailed code, and you can delete your account and records at any time.

What is planned

Bank loans Home, car and personal loans: the instalment schedule worked out from the rate and tenure, each payment split into interest and principal, what is still owed counted in net worth, and room for part-prepayment and floating rates.
The AI phase Ask Alfred in plain words ("How much did I spend on food last month?"), briefings written around your own figures, and entries drafted from messages or statements for you to confirm. Always shown to you first; nothing is changed without a yes.

Under the hood

  • Runs on Cloudflare's free tier (Workers and D1), so it costs next to nothing to keep running.
  • Amounts are stored in whole paise and dates in Indian time, so figures always add up. Everything is checked by an automated test suite before each release.
  • Telegram first, web second, and the AI features are an addition, not a requirement: Alfred is useful without them.
